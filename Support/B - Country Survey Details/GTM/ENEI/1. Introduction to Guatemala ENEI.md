## What is the GTM ENEI?

The Encuesta Nacional de Empleo e Ingresos (ENEI) is Guatemala's National Employment and Income Survey, produced by the Instituto Nacional de Estadistica (INE). It collects household and person information used to describe employment, unemployment, income, and related social and economic conditions. The ENEI records included in the GLD work cover 2018, 2019, 2021, and 2022. The 2018 and 2019 deliveries contain two rounds each; 2021 and 2022 each have one delivery in the files used here.

## What does the GTM ENEI cover?

The ENEI questionnaires cover household and dwelling conditions, demographics, education, training, labor-market activity, job characteristics, income, and other topics. The modules and questionnaire structure vary by year; in particular, the 2022 questionnaire was substantially reorganized. A change in question number or variable name should not be assumed to indicate a change in concept, or vice versa.

The table reports the observations in the GLD harmonized outputs. Household and individual counts are round-level observations, not counts of distinct people or households linked longitudinally across years. Expanded population is the sum of the harmonized `weight` variable.

| Year | Rounds | Household observations | Individual observations | Expanded population |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2018 | 2 | 10,468 | 44,829 | 17,337,078 |
| 2019 | 2 | 10,735 | 45,074 | 17,732,214 |
| 2021 | 1 | 5,790 | 24,319 | 17,207,129 |
| 2022 | 1 | 5,255 | 20,173 | 17,453,776 |

For 2018 and 2019, the two round-specific expansion factors are each divided by two in the pooled output, so the expanded population is the equal-round pooled total rather than the sum of the two round totals. The 2021 and 2022 outputs use the expansion factor for their single delivery without a round divisor.

## Where can the data be found?

The Instituto Nacional de Estadistica (INE) publishes ENEI databases and related documentation on its [official ENEI/ENEIC page](https://www.ine.gob.gt/encuesta-nacional-de-empleo-e-ingresos/). Select the relevant survey year and consult its documentation for the questionnaire, definitions, and release-specific details. Access and use are subject to INE's terms.

## What is the sampling procedure?

The sampling frame, selection stages, strata, primary sampling units, and nonresponse adjustments should be taken from the INE methodology documentation for the specific round. These details are not reconstructed here, and a single design description should not be assumed to apply unchanged to every year. The official INE page links the available year-specific documentation.

## What is the geographic significance level?

The released ENEI data identify three study domains: Urbano Metropolitano, Resto Urbano, and Rural Nacional. These are survey representation domains, not administrative department codes. Users should consult the methodology and results for the relevant year before making claims about statistical precision at a particular geographic level; this introduction does not infer department- or municipality-level significance.

## Other noteworthy aspects

### Rounds and identifiers

The 2018 and 2019 rounds are assembled separately and then appended. A household number reused in another round is not treated as panel linkage; the harmonized household identifier distinguishes round records. The 2021 and 2022 deliveries are each assembled as a single cross-section.

### Harmonization and comparability

The harmonized outputs use the GLD variable schema, but source variables and question routing are not identical across all years. The 2022 questionnaire was redesigned, and its mappings were reviewed against the underlying questionnaire concepts rather than copied by question number alone. In particular, employment screening, labor-force routing, earnings components, and education codes should be interpreted using the documentation for each round.
